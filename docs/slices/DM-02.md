# DM-02 — fixed-module terrain destruction

Owner authorized DM-02 and repository-specific image/animation skills on 2026-10-13. Continue under disclosed partial governance; no merge/release or G1/fun acceptance implied.

## Behavior
Three selectable greybox cases share one ballistic controller: thin wall creates an opening; a breakable upper platform drops a target to a lower floor; a bridge drops a target into a clearly marked deep pit. Amber geometry is breakable; grey geometry stays solid. A module has 2 HP; an accepted projectile strike deals 2, deduplicated by shot_id. State is committed at contact; collider removal is deferred to the safe physics update, not to animation completion.

Fall damage is an experimental candidate: descent from airborne peak <=80 logical px is safe, (80,160) is 1 HP, >=160 is capped at 2 HP. Landing emits once; later independent falls can damage again. Ordinary enemies in a deep pit are defeated once. The Boss policy adapter caps damage at 2 and recovers a surviving target to a safe floor; no Boss encounter/content is implemented or accepted.

The player area has safe permanent floor. No player death, arbitrary pixel destruction, physics debris, barrels, recursive explosions, D20 or enemy turn behavior. R resets the selected case; keys/buttons 1/2/3 change case and clear all in-flight objects. M reduces decoration while retaining actual projectile/character physics, danger and result text.

## Layers
TerrainRules is pure input/result logic; TargetBody measures fall distance and translates native landing; BreakableTerrain owns module state/collider; ProjectileBody translates swept contact. TerrainLab owns case selection and HUD. Decorative fragments have no collider or damage callbacks. Animation cannot decide HP or restore destroyed geometry.
