# DM-01 — physical aiming lab

## Frozen scope
Owner authorized the next slice on 2026-10-13 after reviewing partial governance status. This authorizes DM-01 development and its own Draft PR, not merge/release, full governance adoption, G1 acceptance or subsequent slices.

One fixed 1280x720 greybox battlefield: a moving/jumping player, two six-HP targets, a central wall, a raised platform and a right wall. Hold left mouse to aim and release to launch a gravity-driven projectile. Cursor distance selects power; preview stops at the first collision. Esc cancels without firing. R or the Reset button reloads the scene. Movement is locked during aim and resolution.

Direct impact deals 2 HP and pushes the target. A sufficiently fast horizontal collision with a static wall adds at most 1 HP per target/shot. No damage on resting contact. Missed shots expire; resolution returns to input in at most four seconds. Fixed terrain and contained actors only: no destruction, fall damage, D20, enemy action, profession abilities, saves or progression.

The slice table in GAME_PLAN §12 places destruction in DM-02; §17/§18 examples overlap with that future scope. Follow the explicit slice table for DM-01 and defer breakable pillars/fall damage to DM-02. UI is English prototype copy to avoid claiming unverified CJK font support; localization remains later presentation work.

## Layer ownership
- Domain: ShotRules, pure supplied-coordinate/time transformations and per-shot bounded damage; no scene/OS/time acquisition.
- Combat adapters: player motion, swept ray projectile collision, target movement and static-wall collision translation.
- Presentation: lab construction, pointer/key input, preview, status and transient impact ring. Owns no D20 or progression logic.
- Infrastructure/tooling: exact-engine native runner and development-package creation.

No peer project dependency or framework semantic change. DM-01-I1 integrates the existing game bytes onto main a1068a8, retaining framework pin 2a09d542 and GDScript routing. The bound native validator now verifies both DM-01 test sentinels and the complete five-command sequence. Governance adoption remains partial; this is not full framework compliance.
