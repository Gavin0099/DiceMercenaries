# DM-02 playtest

Extract Windows ZIP and run Play.cmd. Hold/release left mouse to aim/fire; A/D or arrows move, Space jumps, Esc cancels, R resets.

- 1 / WALL: break amber wall; adjust your next shot through its opening. Grey floor/wall remain solid.
- 2 / PLATFORM: aim at amber support surface, not the enemy; destruction makes the enemy fall to the permanent lower floor for up to two HP.
- 3 / DEEP PIT: break amber bridge; ordinary enemy falls into the hatched danger area and is defeated. The player has safe floor on the left.
- M: toggle reduced decorative effects. Confirm that HP, collider removal and falling behavior stay the same.
- Reset and change case mid-flight. No old projectile, fragment or damage should enter the new case.
