# DM-01 playtest

1. Extract the Windows development ZIP completely. Double-click Play.cmd; wait for first-time script import.
2. A/D or left/right: move. Space: jump. Hold left mouse: aim; cursor direction is launch angle, distance sets power. Release: fire.
3. Fire at a low angle: the central wall/ground stops the shot. Try a higher arc; use the first-impact preview.
4. Hit a target and watch its position/HP. Push it into the right wall: direct damage is two, wall damage one at most per shot.
5. Move closer to reach the raised target. Leftward shots may miss; they must not freeze the controls.
6. While aiming, press Esc: no shot spent. Releasing afterward must not fire.
7. R / Reset reloads the entire lab, including during a projectile flight. Both targets return to six HP.
8. Try a resized window. Check preview/mouse alignment and that Reset remains clickable.

Pending Owner feedback: intuitive aiming? understandable misses? readable push/landing? desire to try another approach?
This is one physical lab; D20, destruction, enemy turns and a complete adventure are absent.
